Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using sunlight. It takes place mainly in the leaves, inside tiny structures called chloroplasts.

Chloroplasts contain a green pigment called chlorophyll, which absorbs light energy, mostly from the red and blue parts of sunlight. This energy is used to split water molecules into hydrogen and oxygen.

The overall equation is: carbon dioxide + water + light energy gives glucose + oxygen. The glucose is used by the plant for energy and growth, and the oxygen is released into the air through small pores called stomata.
